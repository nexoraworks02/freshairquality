# Getting freshairquality.com back

**Domain facts (public WHOIS/RDAP lookup, 9 Oct 2026)**
- Registrar: **Hostinger** (HOSTINGER operations, UAB)
- Registered: 16 Oct 2025 · **Expires: 16 Oct 2026** ⚠️
- Status: `clientTransferProhibited` (registrar lock is ON)

Do the steps in this order and keep screenshots of everything.

---

## Step 1: Message the developer (send by WhatsApp AND email, so it's in writing)

> Hi [Developer name],
>
> I'm moving the freshairquality.com domain and website into my own account. Please send me the following by **[date, 2 days from now]**:
>
> 1. **Unlock** the domain freshairquality.com in Hostinger (turn off "Transfer lock").
> 2. Send me the **EPP / transfer authorization code** for freshairquality.com.
> 3. Confirm the **registrant (owner) name and email** on the domain. It should be my business, Fresh Air Quality.
>
> Please **do not let the domain expire.** It expires on **16 October 2026**.
>
> This is my business's domain and I have the right to transfer it. Please confirm that you received this message.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your full name]
> Fresh Air Quality · (647) 367-0164

If they ask for money: you only owe for the domain itself (about $10–20 per year) if you agreed to that. You can pay the renewal yourself once the domain is in your own account.

---

## Step 2: If the developer doesn't reply in 2 days, contact Hostinger directly

Use live chat at hostinger.com → Help, or email. Send:

> Hello Hostinger Support,
>
> I am the owner of the business **Fresh Air Quality** and the domain **freshairquality.com**, which is registered with Hostinger. The domain was set up by a developer who is now refusing to give me access or the transfer (EPP) code. The domain expires on 16 October 2026.
>
> I'm requesting help to regain control of the domain, or to receive the EPP/authorization code so I can transfer it to my own account. I can provide proof of ownership: business registration, ID, invoices, and the business email/phone shown on the website (hello@freshairquality.com, (647) 367-0164).
>
> Please tell me what documents you need.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your full name]

**Proof to collect now:** business registration or HST number, government ID, any invoice or receipt showing you paid for the website or domain, and screenshots of your chats with the developer about the website.

---

## Step 3: If Hostinger can't help (domain is in the developer's personal name)

- File a complaint with ICANN (the organisation that oversees all domain registrars): https://www.icann.org/compliance/complaint
- **Backup plan:** if the domain expires without being renewed, it goes into about 30 days of grace period and then about 30 days of redemption before it's released. Place a **backorder** (DropCatch, SnapNames or GoDaddy Backorder) to try to catch it when it drops.
- **Safety net, do today:** buy `freshairquality.ca` in **your own** account, so the new website has a home no matter what happens.

---

## Once you have the domain
1. Transfer it to **your own** registrar account (Hostinger, Namecheap or Cloudflare), with your own login and email.
2. Turn on **auto-renew** and **two-factor login**.
3. Set up business email (Google Workspace or Zoho Mail), e.g. hello@freshairquality.com.
4. Point the domain to the new website (Vercel). Don't redirect any URLs: the new site already uses the same page addresses (/ontario/, /alberta/ …).
